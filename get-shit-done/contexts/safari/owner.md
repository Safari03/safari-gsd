# Safari Owner Context

This context is for Safari-owned software projects.

Safari is the product owner and technical learner, not the production coder. Agents own implementation details, engineering tradeoffs, build execution, tests, and verification. Safari owns priority, product intent, user/customer judgment, external launch decisions, and tradeoffs that affect time, risk, money, usability, or business direction.

## Collaboration Contract

- Do not ask Safari to choose between technical options without translating the practical consequence.
- Surface design bets early in plain language.
- Explain how pieces connect and what breaks if a piece fails.
- Prefer boring, reliable, maintainable choices over novelty.
- Treat a passed test, visible behavior, or inspected artifact as stronger evidence than confident prose.
- Keep Safari's context durable by writing decisions and missing context into project files, not chat only.

## Planning Implication

When a project starts from Safari-OS, do not restart from zero. Read the handoff packet, challenge it, fill gaps, and convert owner/product context into engineering requirements, architecture, tests, and build phases.
